# Romanian seeds: 1,000-entry review draft

**Status: seeds approved on 2026-11-17.** The 60-module plan and these
1,000 entries are approved. The next step is expanding the dictionary;
the live app still uses the 44-entry pilot until that expansion is published.

Exactly **1,000 unique seed IDs**, in **60 modules** and **200 five-entry
batches**. All 44 pilot IDs are retained. Romanian forms and short English
glosses below explain what the IDs mean; they are not the final dictionary.

See [selection method and references](seed-sources.md) for frequency checks,
scope decisions, word-type totals, and notes for the dictionary expansion.
