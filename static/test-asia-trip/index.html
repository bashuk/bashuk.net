A few things before you go

Less planning. More exploring.

Get around easily.

Pick up an EasyCard in Taipei and a T-money card in Seoul. Keep cash for top-ups and small vendors. Use the MRT in Taipei; Naver Map or KakaoMap is useful for Seoul navigation.

Stay in the right spot.

For a first visit, consider Zhongshan or the Taipei Main Station area in Taipei; Jongno or Euljiro in Seoul. Prioritise a short walk to a metro station over a long list of hotel extras.

Leave a little room.

Carry a compact umbrella, comfortable shoes and cash for markets. Swap days around closures and weather. Book flights between the cities separately and keep airport days light.

This is a suggested itinerary, not a booking schedule. Seven days in each city assumes travel fits around the plan; reduce activities on flight days.
